# Breaker Industries

> Breaker builds Avalon, an AI agent that runs onboard robots so one operator can command a team of drones, ground vehicles and uncrewed vessels by voice over a push-to-talk radio, with no cloud link. Breaker is headquartered in Austin, Texas, with engineering in Sydney, Australia. Founded in 2023, it raised a US$6M seed round led by Bessemer Venture Partners (17 February 2026) and received an AU$1.2M Australian Government Industry Growth Program grant (11 June 2026).

Facts: Company name Breaker Industries, trading as Breaker. Founded 2023. Co-founders: Matthew Buffa (co-CEO, Austin), Michael Irwin (co-CEO, Sydney) and Vanja Videnovic (CTO). Partner: Rheinmetall, integrating Avalon into the Australian Boxer CRV (teaming agreement announced 6 October 2026). Offices: Austin, Texas, USA (Capital Factory); Sydney, NSW, Australia (Cicada Innovations, Eveleigh). Holds AUKUS Authorised User status. Contact: contact@breakerindustries.com. Media: media@breakerindustries.com.
